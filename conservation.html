The animals your gift protects

Three species. One legacy.

Our conservation partner The Boucher Legacy has worked to safeguard Africa’s wildlife since 2012 — across three pillars: prosecution, conservation and education. Your gift backs the front line for these species, in the reserves you visit.

⚖️ Prosecution🌿 Conservation🎓 Education
Founding cause

Rhino

Where it began. The Boucher Legacy funds anti-poaching and the RhODIS DNA database — linking poachers to horns and carcasses — and works with SANParks to collar and monitor individual rhino in the field.

Signature project

Pangolin

The world’s most-trafficked mammal. With the African Pangolin Working Group, the Boucher Legacy rehabilitates pangolins seized from poachers and releases them back into the wild — then keeps monitoring them.

Flagship species

Elephant

Among the species the Boucher Legacy safeguards alongside rhino and pangolin — protecting Africa’s giants and the habitat they share with wild dog, cheetah and lion across the reserves.
