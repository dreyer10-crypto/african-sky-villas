How it works

Three taps. Real impact.

A single QR donor layer, wherever you are — on the lodge wall, in your welcome pack, or on this page. No app to download, no account to make.

1

Tap the QR

Scan the Tap2Protect code at your African Sky stay, or here on the site. It opens straight to giving — nothing to install.

2

Choose a cause

Pick where your gift goes — anti-poaching, wildlife, or the communities around the reserves you’re visiting.

3

Give & see it land

Give any amount. You get a receipt, and a transparent view of where the collective total goes.

One QR, every touchpoint.

Where it goes

Tied to the places you visit.

Your gift supports conservation in the very destinations of your stay — so the trip that brought you close to the wild helps keep it wild.

Kruger · Marloth Park

Anti-poaching & big game

Backing the rangers and wildlife protection that keep the Greater Kruger’s big five thriving.

Zambezi · Victoria Falls

River & wildlife

Protecting the elephants, hippos and habitat along the Zambezi and around Mosi-oa-Tunya.

Cape Coast · Big Bay

Ocean & coastline

Supporting marine and coastal care along the Cape’s beaches and kelp forests.
