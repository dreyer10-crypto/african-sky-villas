The animals your gift protects

Four species. One legacy.

Our conservation partner The Boucher Legacy has safeguarded Africa’s wildlife since 2012 — across three pillars: prosecution, conservation and education. Your gift backs the front line for these species.

⚖️ Prosecution🌿 Conservation🎓 Education
Founding cause

Rhino

Where it began. Anti-poaching and the RhODIS DNA database — linking poachers to horns and carcasses — with SANParks collaring and monitoring rhino in the field.

Signature project

Pangolin

The world’s most-trafficked mammal. With the African Pangolin Working Group, the Boucher Legacy rehabilitates pangolins seized from poachers and releases them back to the wild.

Flagship species

Elephant

Protected along Zambia’s Zambezi and around Mosi-oa-Tunya — safeguarding Africa’s giants and the habitat they share across the reserves.

Endangered

Wild dog

The African wild dog — one of the continent’s most endangered carnivores — is among the species the Boucher Legacy works to protect.
