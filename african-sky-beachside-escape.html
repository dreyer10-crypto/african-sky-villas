The Apartment

Sea air, open plan, and space to unwind.

Set in the secure Seaside Village complex at Big Bay, the apartment opens into a light-filled, open-plan living, dining and kitchen space that spills onto a private patio. Three restful bedrooms and two full bathrooms make it easy for a family or two couples to settle in for a Cape Town stay.
