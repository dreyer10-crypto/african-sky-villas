The African Sky Collection
We're in the business of unforgettable stays.

We're not just handing over keys — we're giving you a home on the edge of the wild. Every stay in the Collection is chosen and run by people who know this corner of Africa like the back of their hand.

From a Zambezi manor house a stone's throw from Victoria Falls, to a self-catering villa where kudu graze past your stoep in Marloth Park, we pair honest hospitality with the safaris, river days and sundowners worth your time.

We truly believe a few days in the bushveld change you — for good.

The wildlife comes to you

Zebra, kudu, giraffe and wildebeest wander past the stoep, elephant come down to the river, and bushbabies visit the stoep at night. In Marloth Park there's nothing between you and the bush — the animals simply drift through, just as our guests love to tell us.

How to book your stay

STEP 1

Discover

Explore our collection of hand-picked stays and bushveld experiences — from a Zambezi manor to a Marloth Park villa on the edge of the Kruger.

STEP 2

Enquire

Tell us your dates, your group and what you dream of. Send the enquiry form, a WhatsApp or an email — a real person replies, usually within a day.

STEP 3

Plan

We shape a stay around you: the right villa, the safaris and river days worth your time, and honest advice on what to expect when.

STEP 4

Confirm

Lock in your dates and tours. Secure your booking by card or EFT, with your full itinerary and directions sent straight to your inbox.

STEP 5

Experience

Arrive to a home that feels like yours. Our team is on call throughout — so you can switch off and let the bushveld do the rest.
